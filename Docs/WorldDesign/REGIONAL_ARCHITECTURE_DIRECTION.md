# Regional architectural direction

Updated 2026-10-15. User direction for upcoming regional rebuilds; design brief only, not applied Unreal changes. Read alongside WORLD_REBUILD_CHECKLIST.md and GOSAIN_KINGS_LANDING_DIRECTION.md. Existing region mapping confirmed from Mapped_World_El_Ruhn_v23.md and Regional_Civilizations_v29.md.

## Kelsin Federation — Zanarkand (dry inland cities)
User correction: Kelsin has NO water. Do not add canals, lakes, waterfronts, ornamental pools or water-based infrastructure. Translate the reference through architecture, elevated streets and magical conduits only.

User reference: Final Fantasy X Zanarkand for capital AND cities. Working interpretation: the living luminous metropolis seen in the opening/Dream Zanarkand, not the ruined pilgrimage destination. Use sweeping curved structures, tall elegant skyline, layered elevated routes, monumental arcs and restrained luminous detailing. Adapt into established arcane manufacturing economy: magical fabrication halls, conduits, workshops and civic districts. Capital gets the grandest skyline; smaller cities share the language at reduced scale. Keep readable occupied streets and buildings beneath spectacle; do not substitute generic crystal spikes or modern office blocks. Reference interpretation remains open to user correction.

## Vylfod Dominion — coastal resort
Existing map places Vylfod southeast/bottom right. Likely requested FFXV reference is Galdin Quay. Use an elegant over-water pavilion as a harbor landmark, long connected piers, supported waterfront decks, open-air dining, shaded promenades, inns, fishing docks and clear sheltered water. Expand this resort vocabulary into functioning coastal towns with homes, markets, service quays and boats. Preserve appropriate tidal/stilt identity. The separately requested floating pleasure city remains a separate placement/design task; do not silently merge it into Vylfod or move it.

## Yozuan Desert — oasis and cliff cities
User requests oasis towns, sandstone structures inspired by ancient Egypt/Mesopotamia, AND settlements built into cliff walls. Provide distinct but related settlement types:
- Oasis capitals/towns: water-supported palm groves, irrigation, shaded courtyards and souks, caravan yards, sandstone gateways/temples and stepped monumental platforms.
- Canyon cities: multi-storey masonry settlements sheltered inside actual cliff alcoves, connected ledges, stairs, terraces, cisterns and routes to the valley. Do not place a row of houses against an uncut cliff or leave floating foundations.
Probable Arizona reference: Montezuma Castle, a Sinagua/Hisatsinom cliff dwelling; identification is an inference, not user-confirmed. Reference spatial integration and sheltered massing, not indiscriminate copying of cultural symbols.
Mesopotamian ziggurats historically used mud brick; combine warm earthen/plastered neighborhoods with the requested sandstone monumental structures as an intentional fantasy palette, not a claim all ancient Mesopotamian buildings were sandstone. Keep oasis settlements lush locally and wider desert legible. Retain remote sanctuary/cult sites as distinct from everyday towns.

## Rabaric Republic — agricultural export powerhouse
User requires port towns and a LARGE processing/manufacturing town with mills, food businesses and bakeries. Existing documents identify Rabaric as the farm nation.
Proposed references: Zaanse Schans for wind-powered processing and merchant settlement; Hamburg Speicherstadt for dense warehouse/quay/canal organization. Adapt to the game's era and anime art, not literal modern machinery or copied nineteenth-century city blocks.
Visible economic chain: fields/orchards -> collection yards/granaries -> milling and pressing -> bakeries, breweries, dairies and preserving halls -> packing warehouses/customs/weigh houses -> export wharves and ships.
Build a major riverside processing city with mill races, watermills/windmills, grain stores, loading courts, cooperages, workers' neighborhoods, food markets and baker streets. Smaller river/coastal ports collect regional produce. Trade roads/canals must connect farms, processors and ports. Distinguish lively public food streets from bulk freight/service yards. Avoid reducing a huge export nation to scattered cottages and decorative windmills.

## Order, consistency and acceptance
Continue Gosain first, then Yozuan, Kelsin, Rabaric, Vylfod/pleasure city per existing order. Record stages so usage interruptions do not lose decisions. El Ruhn remains protected. Do not rebuild all regions simultaneously or alter shared global materials. Reuse suitable complete native assets, prepare region-specific kits where necessary, verify one representative district before expanding. All regions need believable water/road connections, grounded buildings, useful variation and recognisable distant silhouettes. Reference gathering is not implementation or final art acceptance.
