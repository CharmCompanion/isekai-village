# Desert oases, shoreline and cliff ledges — v25

El Ruhn remains the neutral floating country. The mainland desert now has ten irregularly scattered spring-fed oases: five in the greener southern margin and five farther into the desert. Their sizes, orientations and vegetation outlines vary. A deterministic random seed preserves placement between builds; there is no arc layout.

Golden-brown sand extends to the same coastal boundary as the ocean color. Wet sand blends directly into water, while the coastal bench has been lowered. Darker inland settlement indications distinguish these from beaches, snow and green oasis vegetation.

A sandstone escarpment along the desert's eastern interior faces west into the basin. It rises up to roughly 630 m above the surrounding terrain, with two broad ledges. These establish a possible setting for a nomadic cliff community. No named tribe, buildings or population gameplay are invented. A candidate ledge coordinate is recorded in the world-design JSON.
