# Desert canyon cut — v27

The former raised desert escarpment is replaced by a deep diagonal canyon inspired by the broad character of the Grand Canyon. This is an original scenic landform, not a reconstruction of the real location.

The canyon cuts roughly 33 km through the western desert, with opposing walls, variable width, layered warm sandstone, uneven terraces and six curved side ravines. A broad plateau provides the height needed for deep incision while keeping the canyon floor above sea level. The floor is a dry seasonal wash. Surviving broad ledges remain a possible setting for the nomad cliff-community idea.

Oases retain their previous locations where they remain outside the new cut; conflicting sites are moved to nearby eligible ground. Settlement indications avoid the canyon, and routes prefer terrain outside it. El Ruhn and its exact matching opening are preserved.
