# Gosain: King's Landing art direction

Updated 2026-10-15. User explicitly requests Gosain resemble King's Landing closely. This guides the ongoing whole-two-island city rebuild; El Ruhn remains protected.

## Reference target
Use intact, pre-destruction King's Landing: the Dubrovnik-based coastal city, tightly packed warm roofs, pale weathered masonry, commanding hilltop fortress, monumental religious precinct, and massive coastal defenses. Preserve IsekaiVillage's anime rendering and Gosain's existing two-island geography and connecting bridge. This is a close architectural/compositional adaptation, not a claim of an exact map replica.

Research: RSP's production account explains Dubrovnik as the city foundation and the importance of believable architectural weight and scale. Reference establishing shots show the relationship between roofscape, enclosing walls, water, and elevated citadel.

## Required design translation
1. Both islands must read as continuous urban fabric within their coastal walls. Open space is purposeful streets, small courts, markets, docks and monumental squares, not broad empty lawns or random house scatter.
2. Grade buildable hills into irregular connected terraces. Buildings climb the slopes; retaining walls, stair lanes and switchback streets explain elevation. Avoid concentric terrace rings and steep slopes with houses pasted onto them.
3. Give the imperial citadel one dominant elevated silhouette above a lower residential roofscape. Build a coherent fortress complex with courtyards, towers and substantial bases rather than isolated spikes. Keep a separate religious landmark/precinct.
4. Use close-set two-to-five-storey buildings as a proposed starting range, varied frontage widths, joined street edges, irregular courtyards and narrow shaded alleys. Building sizes must remain consistent with streets, gates and bridge.
5. Use warm limestone/plaster, muted terracotta roofs, aged stone, restrained banners and lively market accents. Adapt these to anime lighting and readable material detail. Do not change global lighting or shared materials.
6. Make a clear gate-to-bridge-to-market-to-citadel route; branches form dense neighborhoods. Bridge gates need room to transition into streets without opening into empty hillsides.
7. Harbor edges need quays, warehouses, stairs and modest waterfront activity. Different districts should imply centuries of additions and repairs.

## User correction — 2026-10-16: connected urban architecture
Gosain must NOT use the stock detached house designs as its final architecture. The Gosain39 house pass is superseded as an art target and must not be expanded.

Required: large connected multi-storey building blocks, adjoining/shared walls, stepped building heights, interconnected narrow alleys and corridors, covered passages, inner courtyards, stairs and layered terraces. Urban blocks must read as continuous inhabited architecture rather than scattered cottages or repeated freestanding houses. Develop original Gosain block geometry; suitable pack textures and small details may support it, but recoloring or scaling stock houses does not satisfy the correction.

Next art checkpoint: author and inspect a complete representative connected block with legible entrances and through-routes, varied roofs and meaningful internal space. Verify at street level, aerial view and reverse view before applying this language across both islands. Replace the temporary stock-house population in controlled districts after the replacement block is validated. Retain the terrain, fortifications and bridge work where appropriate.

## Superseding architecture correction — 2026-10-16
The user rejected the Gosain41 arcade/courtyard prototype: Gosain is Vatican-like masonry, not Venice or a timber-rafter town. Use monumental stone institutional architecture as the older layer, overwhelmed by densely adjoining, irregular multi-storey additions. The overcrowded Asian city reference describes density and loss of daylight in deep lanes, not a request to copy modern materials.

Existing roads determine the building footprints. Do not place a formal repeated block then run new roads around it. Preserve primary routes; subdivide the land between them into shared-wall buildings, constricted lanes, small light wells, enclosed upper passages and uneven roof terraces. Avoid exposed rafters, repetitive cottage roofs, open stilt floors and a symmetrical arcade as the dominant motif. Organic means an accumulated urban history expressed through circulation, parcel shapes, party walls and additions, not random deformation.

Gosain42 supersedes the unplaced41 prototype. It develops two whole existing street blocks west of the precinct. Do not expand this language across both islands until native views have been inspected; record actual application and remaining limitations in GOSAIN42_CHECKPOINT.md.
